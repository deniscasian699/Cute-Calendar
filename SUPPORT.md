## 🔔 Notifications Not Working?

If morning reminders are not arriving, this is almost always caused by battery optimization settings on your device.

**Steps to fix:**

1. Open **Android Settings → Apps → Cute Calendar → Battery**
2. Set battery usage to **Unrestricted** or **Not optimized**
3. On Samsung: Settings → Device Care → Battery → Background usage limits → remove Cute Calendar
4. On Xiaomi/MIUI: Settings → Apps → Manage Apps → Cute Calendar → Battery saver → No restrictions
5. On Huawei/EMUI: Settings → Battery → App launch → Cute Calendar → Manage manually → enable all three toggles
6. On OnePlus: Settings → Battery → Battery optimization → Cute Calendar → Don't optimize

After adjusting, re-enable the Morning Reminder in the app.

## 💳 Purchase & Billing Issues

All purchases are processed by **Google Play**. For refunds, billing problems, or subscription management:

1. Open the app → tap the crown icon → **Manage Subscription**
2. Or visit: [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)
3. For refunds, contact Google Play support directly

**To restore a previous purchase:**
Open the app → Premium screen → **Restore Purchase**

## 🚫 How to Remove Ads

Ads are shown to free users to support development. You can remove all ads with any of these purchases:

- ⭐ **Premium Lifetime** — one-time unlocks all features
- 🚫 **Remove Ads** — one-time, removes ads only
- 🏆 **Supporter Monthly / Yearly** — subscription, removes ads + custom icons

All purchases are available inside the app on the Premium screen.
